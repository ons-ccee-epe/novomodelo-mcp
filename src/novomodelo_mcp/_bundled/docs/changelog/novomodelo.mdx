## 0.22.0

Released 2026-11-24.

### Added

- `modeling.inflow_residual.distribution` in `config.json`: `"lognormal_3p"`
  draws inflow residuals from a three-parameter lognormal bounded below at zero
  inflow; default `"normal"`. Refused with `external` or `historical` inflows,
  `enumerated` selection, `historical_residuals` stages or `scenario_id` nodes.
- `system/hydros.json` accepts `tailrace_flow` (`own_outflow_factor`,
  `outflow_influences`, `incremental_inflow_influences`); bad entries are
  refused and `validate` warns without FPHA. `simulation/hydros` adds
  `tailrace_flow_m3s`, `tailrace_level_m` and `net_head_m`.
- `system/hydros.json` accepts `spillage` with `min_spillage_m3s` and
  `max_spillage_m3s` (m3/s), bounding the plant's spillage in every block;
  `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet` values replace them. Negative values
  and a minimum above the maximum are refused.
- `training.selection.first_iteration_trial_storage` in `config.json`:
  `"storage_grid"` builds the first iteration's cuts at an even grid of
  storage levels instead of the forward passes' storage. `"forward"`, the
  default, keeps the earlier behaviour.

### Changed

- Results change for FPHA hydros declaring `tailrace_flow`: `gamma_s` in
  `system/fpha_hyperplanes.parquet` and the `fpha_hyperplanes` output is the
  lateral-flow slope per m3/s (spill slope if none).
- Results change for hydros with `fpha_config.source: "computed"`: planes keep
  their fitted spill slope (`gamma_s`), so a spilling plant generates less. It
  may again turbine water at 0 MW in `simulation/hydros`, as before 0.21.0.
  `novomodelo validate` now warns only on planes below 0 MW without spillage.

### Removed

- **BREAKING:** `post_study_stages.json` with a `stages` key is refused. Migrate
  each deck with `python3 scripts/migrate/post_study_stages.py <deck-dir>`.
- **BREAKING:** `estimation.correlation_target` and
  `training.scenario_source.openings.moment_matching` in `config.json` are
  refused; delete them. `training/model_provenance.json` no longer records
  `inflow.correlation_target` and `inflow.opening_tree_moment_matching`.
- **BREAKING:** `spillage_discretization_points` and `max_planes_per_hydro` in
  `fpha_config` of `system/hydro_production_models.json` are refused; delete
  them. Results do not change.
- `novomodelo run` no longer warns when `lp_row_violations` in
  `solver/iterations.parquet` is nonzero; the column is still written.

### Fixed

- `scenario_source.inflow.scheme` `"historical"` and `"external"` refused a
  zero inflow standard deviation at a hydro and season (`V2.3`, `V3.7`). That
  season now takes the model's deterministic inflow, not the recorded one.
  `"external"` also refused a constant inflow column under an AR(p > 0) model.
- `novomodelo validate` warned that the LP prices negative values in
  `scenarios/inflow_history.parquet` and `initial_conditions.json`
  `recent_observations`. It said so whatever
  `modeling.inflow_non_negativity.method` was set to.

## 0.21.0

Released 2026-11-16.

### Added

- `constraints/generic_constraints.json` accepts `hydro_outflow_volume(h)`,
  `hydro_diversion_volume(h)` and `pumping_volume(station)`, stage volumes in
  hm3. Their constraints may add only volume and storage terms, with no block
  argument, `per_stage_block` parameter or bounds row with a `block_id`.
- `constraints/thermal_bounds.parquet` accepts `cost_per_mwh` on a row with a
  `block_id`, pricing that block's generation. Such rows on a thermal with
  `anticipated_config` are still refused.
- `estimation.history_years` in `config.json`, as `{"from", "to"}`, limits the
  years of `scenarios/inflow_history.parquet` the inflow model is estimated
  from; absent, every year is used. A range reaching outside the file's years
  is refused.
- `novomodelo validate` warns when `hydro.fpha_violation_cost` is at or below
  the largest deficit cost plus `generation_violation_below_cost`.
- `novomodelo validate` warns when a hydro's minimum storage in
  `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet` rises above the previous stage's, which
  can make a stage infeasible.

### Changed

#### Case files

- **BREAKING:** `curtailment_cost` in `system/non_controllable_sources.json`
  must be 0 or more. It now accepts 0 there, in `penalties.json` and in
  `constraints/penalty_overrides_ncs.parquet`, as does `turbined_cost` in
  `constraints/penalty_overrides_hydro.parquet`.
- **BREAKING:** an input Parquet file with a column novomodelo does not read is
  refused, naming the closest known column. Remove the column, as
  `residual_std_ratio` in `scenarios/inflow_ar_coefficients.parquet` and
  `reference_volume_hm3` in `system/hydro_energy_productivity.parquet`.
- `post_study_stages.json` accepts `thermal_stages` and `water_bucket_stages`,
  the second required in full on a travel-time deck without `stages`. `stages`,
  and a travel-time deck without the file, warn as deprecated: migrate with
  `python3 scripts/migrate/post_study_stages.py <deck-dir>`.
- `system/lines.json` and `system/thermals.json` are optional: a case without
  them validates and runs as a study with no lines or no thermals.

#### Inflow model

- **BREAKING:** the AR orders and coefficients estimated from
  `scenarios/inflow_history.parquet` change under both
  `estimation.order_selection` values. Results change.
- **BREAKING:** studies whose `stages.json` splits an inflow-lag period over
  stages, or steps lags from months to quarters, give different results when
  cuts carry inflow-lag terms; retrain them. Results are exact only where the
  partial inflow is path-independent; a month-to-quarter stage warns once.
- PAR(p) folding: the inflow model leaves the stage LP. Each stage's inflow is
  computed from its lags before the solve, so the LP drops the lag columns and
  inflow rows and is smaller. Results change. A 0.20.0 policy is refused at
  load; retrain it.

#### Hydro plants

- **BREAKING:** `penalties.json` must declare `hydro.fpha_violation_cost`
  ($/MWh, > 0); a `penalties.json` without it is refused. Add the key to
  existing cases. It can also be set per plant in `system/hydros.json` and
  per stage in `constraints/penalty_overrides_hydro.parquet`.
- **BREAKING:** FPHA planes with `fpha_config.source: "computed"` are fitted
  over the plant's `min_storage_hm3` to `max_storage_hm3`, which
  `system/hydro_geometry.parquet` must cover or the case is refused. Extend
  the geometry. Results change where the two ranges differ.
- **BREAKING:** a storage bound in `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet` outside
  the reservoir's range in `system/hydros.json` is refused, as is a stage whose
  minimum storage exceeds its maximum. Keep each stage's bounds within the
  reservoir's `min_storage_hm3` to `max_storage_hm3`.
- An FPHA hydro with `max_generation_mw` 0 in `system/hydros.json` needs no rows
  in `system/fpha_hyperplanes.parquet` and is modeled at zero productivity. The
  "no turbine capacity" warning names `max_turbined_m3s` and
  `max_generation_mw`. Results change.

#### Policy

- Boundary cuts read on a bucket calendar other than their training one are
  repriced. In-transit water is priced by the target bucket's hours, and water
  bound for a hydro the policy has no buckets for at that hydro's storage value
  (`filled_from_storage` in `novomodelo validate --json`). Results change.
- For a migrated deck with a travel-time arc, the transit-bucket slots in
  `policy/cuts/*.bin` past the study horizon are now dated, so a later study
  loading that policy as a boundary reads them.

#### Results and outputs

- Training and simulation results change: each stage LP solves the same model
  with fewer rows and columns.

### Fixed

#### Case files

- `novomodelo validate` refused a `stages.json` whose stages of different
  lengths share a `season_id`, each the season's whole period or a slice of
  it. Weeks that name their month, in a study where another year has that
  month as one stage, are such a case.

#### Inflow model

- Sampled training gave results that depended on `--threads` and on the MPI
  rank count when an inflow-lag period spans several stages, as weeks within a
  month or a quarter built from months. Results change for those studies.
- Estimation from `scenarios/inflow_history.parquet` aborted the run when a
  fitted month implied a negative noise variance. That month's order is now
  reduced and listed as `negative_residual_variance` in
  `output/stochastic/fitting_report.json`.
- Validation's warnings on inflow observations per season missed
  `scenarios/inflow_history.parquet` years outside the study horizon and counted
  rows covering only part of a season. They now count the observations
  estimation fits from.

#### Hydro plants

- FPHA plants whose planes cannot be met no longer make the stage infeasible.
  The excess is priced at `hydro.fpha_violation_cost` and reported as
  `fpha_violation_mw` in `simulation/hydros` and `fpha_violation_cost` (inside
  `hydro_violation_cost`) in `simulation/costs`. Results change where it binds.
- `max_generation_mw` or `max_turbined_m3s` of 0 in a block made a hydro or unit
  group turbine water at 0 MW and charged its minima. It now spills that water,
  and `training/hydro_models.json` lists it under `frozen_turbining`. Results
  change.
- A hydro with `fpha_config.source: "computed"` could turbine water at 0 MW in
  `simulation/hydros` while it spilled within its spillage fit range. Results
  change. `novomodelo validate` now warns when a plane in
  `system/fpha_hyperplanes.parquet` falls below 0 MW at zero turbining.
- `evaporation_m3s` in `simulation/hydros` could take the opposite sign to the
  month's `evaporation.coefficients_mm` in `system/hydros.json`. It added water
  in a month of net evaporation or removed it in a month of net rainfall.
  Results change.
- A negative `water_withdrawal_m3s` in `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet`
  could add water through `water_withdrawal_violation_neg_m3s`. A withdrawal
  set before a plant's `entry_stage_id` could make the stage infeasible.
  Results change.

#### Results and outputs

- `storage_violation_cost` and `filling_target_cost` in `simulation/costs`
  were always 0. They now report the cost of a filling plant's soft
  minimum-storage and filling-target slacks.

## 0.20.0

Released 2026-11-03.

### Added

- `training/model_provenance.json` records `inflow.correlation_target` and
  `inflow.opening_tree_moment_matching`, and the Python `StochasticSummary`
  and `ProvenanceReport` carry them as `correlation_target` and
  `opening_tree_moment_matching`.
- `novomodelo run` under `mpiexec` checks that every rank built the same opening
  tree and exits 4 when they differ.
- `novomodelo-python` is published with an sdist next to the wheels. Where no
  wheel fits, such as Linux with glibc older than 2.34 (RHEL 8, Ubuntu 20.04),
  pip builds the sdist, given a Rust toolchain, cmake and a C++ compiler.
- `lp_row_violations` in `solver/iterations.parquet`; `novomodelo run` warns if
  nonzero.

### Changed

- **BREAKING:** the spatial correlation estimated from history now comes from
  the standardized historical inflows instead of the PAR residuals. The new
  `estimation.correlation_target` in `config.json` selects it; `"residuals"`
  restores the earlier results.
- **BREAKING:** generated opening trees are now moment-matched: at each stage,
  every hydro's inflow openings have mean 0 and the variance of its correlated
  noise (1 unless factoring the correlation clips a negative eigenvalue). The
  new `training.scenario_source.openings.moment_matching` in `config.json`
  selects it; `"none"` restores the earlier results.
- **BREAKING:** a stage in `stages.json` with more openings than the first stage
  of its noise group (consecutive stages with the same `season_id` in one year)
  is refused. Lower its `num_openings` to the first stage's count. Results
  change where a group's opening count drops and rises again.
- `training.solver.forward.use_warm_start` defaults to `true` again, so forward
  solves start warm; results change. Simulation solves stay cold; set
  `simulation.solver.use_warm_start: true` to opt in.
- Under `mpiexec`, only rank 0 prints warnings; every rank prints its errors.

### Removed

- Releases no longer include the `novomodelo-mcp` placeholder binary, and its
  installers and archives were removed from the earlier releases. The MCP server
  is the separate `novomodelo-mcp` Python package.
- **BREAKING:** `training.solver.forward.refactor_error_tolerance`,
  `training.solver.backward.refactor_error_tolerance` and
  `simulation.solver.refactor_error_tolerance` are refused; delete the key.

### Fixed

- Training under `mpiexec` with external scenario files
  (`scenarios/external_inflow_scenarios.parquet` and the other external
  libraries) holding fewer scenarios than `num_openings` aborted the run with
  an MPI error.
- `training/model_provenance.json` and the run summary reported a correlation
  read from `scenarios/correlation.json` as `estimated`. They now report
  `user_file`.
- A policy checkpoint in `policy.path` or `policy.boundary` without its empty
  `basis/` directory, which zip tools drop, failed to load.
- **BREAKING:** HiGHS: balances recomputed from outputs could fail to close and
  cuts could carry wrong duals; results change. Retrain policies trained with
  HiGHS.

## 0.19.1

Released 2026-10-26.

### Added

- `fpha_config.spillage_fit_max_m3s` and `fpha_config.turbined_fit_max_m3s` in
  `system/hydro_production_models.json` set the upper end of the spillage and
  turbined-flow ranges a computed FPHA is fit over. Absent, the ranges are
  unchanged.

### Changed

- **BREAKING:** an `initial_conditions.json` storage outside the hydro's
  storage range is refused at load. It used to be clamped silently. A `filling`
  hydro listed under `storage`, or a non-filling one under `filling_storage`,
  is refused too.
- The spillage coefficients of computed FPHA planes change: they are now fit
  over the points where each plane binds.

### Fixed

- The initial storage is no longer moved into the first stage's
  `min_storage_hm3` and `max_storage_hm3` from
  `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet`, which bound the end of that stage; the
  move added or removed water before the first solve. `novomodelo validate`
  warns when the initial storage lies outside those bounds.
- A `max_storage_hm3` in `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet` below a hydro's
  storage no longer makes a stage before its `entry_stage_id` or after its
  `exit_stage_id` infeasible.
- A hydro with computed FPHA on some stages and another model on others, such
  as constant productivity 0.0 on an outage stage, is no longer refused.

## 0.19.0

Released 2026-10-20.

### Added

- `training.cut_selection.selection.lookback_iterations` for `level1` keeps
  the trial states of only the rows added in the last `lookback_iterations`
  iterations, which bounds their memory.
- `simulation.cut_set`, `all` or `active`, chooses which rows of each stage
  the simulation loads.
- Generic-constraint expressions accept `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, parentheses and
  unary minus over numbers and `@parameters`, on both sides of the relation.
  They must stay linear in LP variables.
- A scalar parameter in `constraints/generic_parameters.json` may be
  `kind: "expression"`, a formula over other parameters.
- Stored-energy parameters in MWh: `max_stored_energy_mwh`,
  `integrated_accumulated_productivity_mwh_per_hm3` and
  `integrated_equivalent_productivity_mwh_per_hm3`. Price the slack of a
  constraint written with them with `"penalty_basis": "per_unit"`.
- `integrated_equivalent_productivity_mw_per_m3s` in
  `system/hydro_energy_productivity.parquet` sets a plant's useful-range mean
  productivity for the stage outright.
- A `diversion` channel in `system/hydros.json` may leave out `max_flow_m3s`,
  or set it to `null`, for a channel with no capacity limit.
- A resumed run restores the `level1` trial states, the binding counters of
  `max_active_per_stage` and `dynamic`, and one solver basis per forward pass
  and node. A warm-started `level1` run restores the trial states, and a warm
  start restores the bases of a policy trained with a selection method.

### Changed

- **BREAKING:** `level1` keeps exactly the cuts that own a stored trial state:
  the cut with the highest value at each state. Results change for every study
  that uses `level1`. Retrain its policies.
- **BREAKING:** `simulation.cut_set` defaults to `all`, so a run with `level1`
  or `max_active_per_stage` simulates with every row training added,
  deactivated and evicted ones included. Set it to `active` for the earlier
  behaviour. Runs without selection or a cap, and `dynamic` runs, are
  unchanged.
- **BREAKING:** `row_activity_tolerance` defaults to 1e-6 instead of 0, so a
  run with `max_active_per_stage` or `dynamic` that omits it can evict and seed
  other rows. Set it to 0 for the earlier behaviour.
- **BREAKING:** `violation_tolerance` of `dynamic` is a relative factor, absent
  by default. Runs can return different cuts and bounds, and a value written
  for the earlier absolute tolerance can fail at load: delete the key or raise
  it.
- **BREAKING:** a resumed run continues as the uninterrupted run would: every
  cut keeps its original position, and `level1` and `max_active_per_stage`
  treat the reloaded cuts as their own. A capped resume used to count only the
  cuts added after it. Under `warm_start`, `level1` selects a loaded cut its
  source scored like a generated one.
- `max_active_per_stage` caps only the rows the current run added, not loaded
  ones.
- **BREAKING:** the cut-selection config is checked more strictly. A zero in
  `check_frequency`, `lookback_iterations`, `start_iteration`,
  `candidate_recency` or `max_added_per_round`, a `selection` given as an
  array, and `max_active_per_stage` together with `dynamic` or with
  `training.selection.method = enumerated` are refused at load.
- **BREAKING:** policy checkpoints are written with `format_version` 4, and
  version-3 checkpoints are refused. Re-run the program that produced the
  policy. Cut values are stored in the run's scaled units, so a resume with the
  same `modeling.cost_scale_factor` is exact.
- **BREAKING:** a run that fails writes its partial policy to
  `<policy.path>.partial` and leaves the last whole checkpoint in
  `policy.path`.
- `policy.mode = resume` is refused, with a message naming what is missing,
  when the checkpoint lacks the `level1` trial states or binding counters the
  run needs, holds a shorter lookback window, an incomplete basis store or
  another `training.selection.forward_passes`, or when the rows of
  `policy.boundary` differ from its terminal pool.
- A `dynamic` run that simulates with `simulation.cut_set = all` is refused
  when the policy it loads holds inactive rows that `all` would include. Set
  `simulation.cut_set` to `active` or use `level1`.
- **BREAKING:** `training/cut_selection/iterations.parquet` has one row per
  `(iteration, pool_id)`, `cuts_in_pool` replaces `cuts_populated`, and
  `pool_id` and `node_id` join `stage_id`. Update readers of the file.
- **BREAKING:** `exports.states` writes each pool's stored trial states at the
  pool's state dimension, for every selection setting. The per-stage
  visited-states export at full dimension is removed.
- **BREAKING:** a refused policy loaded only to simulate is reported as
  `SimulationPolicyIncompatible`, by `novomodelo validate --json` and
  `novomodelo.io.validate`, no longer as `WarmStartIncompatible`. Filter on
  both kinds to keep the previous behaviour.
- **BREAKING:** `system/hydro_energy_productivity.parquet` must carry the
  `integrated_equivalent_productivity_mw_per_m3s` column, and
  `equivalent_productivity_mw_per_m3s` sets only the reference-point
  productivity. A plant with VHA geometry now integrates its useful-range mean
  productivity even with that override, so its stored-energy tags and outputs
  change where the two differed. To keep a study's values, write the override
  into the new column too; otherwise add the column as all-NULL.
- A plant whose geometry gives a non-positive useful-range mean head is
  rejected at setup even with an `equivalent_productivity_mw_per_m3s` override.
- **BREAKING:** the `linearized_head` production model is removed: it always
  ran as `constant_productivity`, which gives the same results. `model` and
  `default_model` accept only `constant_productivity` and `fpha`; any other
  name used to run silently as constant productivity.
- **BREAKING:** a positive `max_diversion_m3s` in
  `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet` on a hydro with no `diversion` channel,
  and a negative `diversion.max_flow_m3s`, are rejected at load. Before, the
  diverted water left the system without reaching any plant.
- **BREAKING:** an enabled generic-constraint slack must declare
  `slack.penalty_basis`: `per_hour` charges every hour the row covers, as
  before, and `per_unit` charges once per unit of violation. Add
  `"penalty_basis": "per_hour"` to every enabled slack to keep a study's costs.
  A disabled slack that sets `penalty` or `penalty_basis`, and a field in
  `constraints/generic_parameters.json` that does not belong to the entry's
  kind, are rejected.
- A generic constraint written without a relational operator drops a variable
  whose coefficient is exactly zero. A row left with only stage-level terms
  collapses to one stage-level row, so under `per_unit` a violation costs the
  penalty once instead of once per block.
- x86_64 Linux builds from source, `cargo install` included, and the MPI
  release archive no longer enable AVX2 and FMA, and qhull, HiGHS and CLP
  compile without FMA on every target. Results no longer depend on the build
  host or the compiler's default target, and can differ in the last bits from
  0.18.1's, on aarch64 too. Before, a compiler that targets FMA by default, such as the
  GCC of Ubuntu 26.04, could fit an FPHA plane with a storage coefficient just
  below zero, which loading the planes back as precomputed rejected.
- **BREAKING:** the Linux CLI archives are built on Ubuntu 24.04 and need
  glibc 2.38 or newer. They no longer run on Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or
  RHEL 9. The Python wheels keep their manylinux tags.

### Removed

- **BREAKING:** the `lml1` and `domination` values of
  `training.cut_selection.selection.method` (use `level1`) and `level1`'s
  `tie_tolerance` (delete the key). A config that names them fails at load.

### Fixed

- A plant's `min_turbined_m3s` or `min_generation_mw` in
  `constraints/hydro_bounds.parquet` had no effect, and its
  `max_turbined_m3s` or `max_generation_mw` capped each bus's share of a plant
  whose unit groups sit on several buses, not the total. Both now bind on the
  plant's total.
- A lazy solve under `dynamic` that reached the round cap returned a relaxed
  result.
- `cuts_removed` in `training/convergence.parquet` was always 0, and
  `cuts_active` and `row_pool.peak_active` ignored evictions and
  reactivations.
- Training aborted when `max_active_per_stage` was below the number of cuts
  loaded by `policy.boundary`.
- A generic constraint whose terms land on the same LP column, such as a
  repeated variable, aborted the run when HiGHS loaded the LP.
- A checkpoint over the 2 GiB limit of the policy format, or one a load would
  reject, is refused when written instead of being written unreadable.
- A basis-store decode failure at the end of a multi-rank run left the other
  ranks blocked.
- Under MPI, the backward `wait` and `bwd_load_imbalance_ms` left out the time
  a rank spends waiting for the other ranks.

## 0.18.1

Released 2026-10-14.

### Changed

- **BREAKING:** the default of `modeling.inflow_non_negativity.method` is now
  `truncation_with_penalty` (was `penalty`). Set `"penalty"` to keep the old
  default.
- **BREAKING:** `truncation` and `truncation_with_penalty` follow Larroyd et al.
  (2022). The PAR inflow is no longer raised to zero: it stays raw, may be
  negative, and carries into the next stage's lag. A per-hydro correction lifts
  the inflow that enters the water balance to zero. Under `truncation` it equals
  the shortfall and costs nothing; under `truncation_with_penalty` it lies
  between zero and the shortfall and is priced by
  `hydro.inflow_nonnegativity_cost` in `penalties.json`.
  `incremental_inflow_m3s` reports the raw inflow and
  `inflow_nonnegativity_slack_m3s` the correction. Results change for studies
  whose inflow draws go negative. Under either method the lower bound can exceed
  the upper bound, so a `gap` stopping rule can report convergence too early.
- Under `penalty` and `truncation_with_penalty`, the inflow slack of a plant
  that has not started filling enters the water balance of the plant that
  receives its inflow; it used to enter none.
- Under the truncation methods, a generic constraint's `hydro_inflow` term
  includes the correction, so it matches the inflow in the water balance.
- HiGHS forward and simulation solves start cold by default; backward solves
  still warm-start. Set `"use_warm_start": true` in `training.solver.forward` or
  `simulation.solver` to warm-start that phase. Retries keep the phase's
  setting. A cold solve can stop at a different, equally optimal vertex, so
  simulated duals and the final lower bound may differ slightly from 0.18.0.
- `novomodelo run` writes its report (banner, progress and summaries) to stdout;
  warnings and errors stay on stderr. Scripts and job logs that read the report
  from stderr must read stdout. `--color` applies to both streams.
- The training gap is printed with 5 decimals instead of 1.

### Fixed

- `truncation` no longer produces an infeasible LP when an inflow's standard
  deviation is zero.
- `novomodelo run` under MPI no longer panics and hangs when the forward passes
  do not divide evenly across ranks.
- A rank that panics under MPI aborts the whole job with exit code 101, instead
  of leaving the other ranks waiting until the job is killed.
- Under MPI, the training summary and `solve_stats` in `training/metadata.json`
  no longer multiply the LP-solve counts and the forward solve time by the
  number of ranks.

## 0.18.0

Released 2026-10-07.

### Changed

- **BREAKING:** the product is renamed. The CLI is `novomodelo`
  (`novomodelo-mpi` for the MPI build), the Rust crates are `novomodelo-*`, the
  Python package is `novomodelo-python` (`import novomodelo`), and environment
  variables such as `COBRE_TCP_COORDINATOR` are now `NOVOMODELO_TCP_COORDINATOR`.
  Everything else behaves as in Cobre 0.18.0.
